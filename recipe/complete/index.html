Vegan Chocolate Cake

This is Chloe Coscarelli’s Celebration Cake, which can be found here. My friends and family members (who do not have dietary restrictions!) have asked for this recipe. I’ve exclusively used this cake recipe for almost a decade. No eggs and no dairy.

Illustration by Grace Lee

Prep time: 25 min

Bake time: 30 min

Yields: 16–24 slices

Makes two nine-inch layers. Double the recipe for four layers.

Make-Ahead Tip: Cake layers can be made in advance and frozen, unfrosted, for up to 1 month. Thaw cakes and frost before serving.

Ingredients

  • *For a gluten-free alternative, substitute gluten-free all-purpose flour plus 1½ teaspoons xanthan gum.
